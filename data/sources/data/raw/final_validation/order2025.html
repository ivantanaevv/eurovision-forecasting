    Eurovision 2025 Grand Final running order released

    Vincent Mazoyer Follow on X May 16, 2025Last Updated: May 16, 2025
    0 1,125
    Facebook X Reddit Messenger Messenger WhatsApp Telegram

    The full running order of the Grand Final of Eurovision 2025 has been released! You can find the details below:

    1. 🇳🇴 Norway – Kyle Alessandro – “Lighter”
    2. 🇱🇺 Luxembourg – Laura Thorn – “La poupée monte le son”
    3. 🇪🇪 Estonia – Tommy Cash – “Espresso macchiato”
    4. 🇮🇱 Israel – Yuval Raphael – “New Day Will Rise”
    5. 🇱🇹 Lithuania – Katarsis – “Tavo akys”
    6. 🇪🇸 Spain – Melody – “Esa diva”
    7. 🇺🇦 Ukraine – Ziferblat – “Bird of Pray”
    8. 🇬🇧 United Kingdom – Remember Monday – “What the Hell Just Happened?”
    9. 🇦🇹 Austria – JJ – “Wasted Love”
    10. 🇮🇸 Iceland – VÆB – “Róa”
    11. 🇱🇻 Latvia – Tautumeitas – “Bur man laimi”
    12. 🇳🇱 The Netherlands – Claude – “C’est la vie”
    13. 🇫🇮 Finland – Erika Vikman – “Ich komme”
    14. 🇮🇹 Italy – Lucio Corsi – “Volevo essero un duro”
    15. 🇵🇱 Poland – Justyna Steczkowska – “Gaja”
    16. 🇩🇪 Germany – Abor & Tynna – “Baller”
    17. 🇬🇷 Greece – Klavdia – “Asteromata”
    18. 🇦🇲 Armenia – Parg – “Survivor”
    19. 🇨🇭 Switzerland – Zoë Më – “Voyage”
    20. 🇲🇹 Malta – Miriana Conte – “Serving”
    21. 🇵🇹 Portugal – NAPA – “Deslocado”
    22. 🇩🇰 Denmark – Sissal – “Hallucination”
    23. 🇸🇪 Sweden – KAJ – “Bara bada bastu”
    24. 🇫🇷 France – Louane – “Maman”
    25. 🇸🇲 San Marino – Gabry Ponte – “Tutta l’Italia”
    26. 🇦🇱 Albania – Shkodra Elektronika – “Zjerm”

    There’ll be at least two commercial breaks during the show, although their precise timing is yet unsure. We will be able to confirm them during the first dress rehearsal.

    What do you think of this running order? Who are the winners and losers of these choices? And now that we have all the performances and the final running order, the most important question: who will win Eurovision 2025? Tell us in the comments below or on social media at @escxtra!
