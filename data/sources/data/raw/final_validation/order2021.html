Eurovision 2021: Grand Final Running Order Announced

byGerry Avelino
21 May 2021
2 minute read

The running order for the grand final of the Eurovision Song Contest 2021 has been revealed.

Twenty-six countries take to the stage on Saturday for the Grand Final of the Eurovision Song Contest 2021, live from the Rotterdam Ahoy arena. The 10 qualifiers from the first semi-final join the 10 qualifiers from the second semi-final and the 6 automatic qualifiers at the big show.

Each qualified act from the semi-finals drew which half they would perform in during the post-show press conferences, while each of the Big 5 (France, Spain, Germany, United Kingdom, Italy) drew during their press conferences after their rehearsals earlier this week.

The Netherlands, as the host country, drew their spot during the Head of Delegation meeting in 2020 before that year’s contest, which was canceled. The Eurovision Reference Group decided that the Netherlands would keep that spot in the running order for 2021.

The running order was decided by the contest producers and approved by the EBU through Executive Supervisor Martin Österdahl.

Here is the running order for Saturday night:

1. 🇨🇾 Cyprus
2. 🇦🇱 Albania
3. 🇮🇱 Israel
4. 🇧🇪 Belgium
5. 🇷🇺 Russia
6. 🇷🇺 Malta
7. 🇵🇹 Portugal
8. 🇷🇸 Serbia
9. 🇬🇧 United Kingdom
10. 🇬🇷 Greece
11. 🇨🇭 Switzerland
12. 🇮🇸 Iceland
13. 🇪🇸 Spain
14. 🇲🇩 Moldova
15. 🇩🇪 Germany
16. 🇫🇮 Finland
17. 🇧🇬 Bulgaria
18. 🇱🇹 Lithuania
19. 🇺🇦 Ukraine
20. 🇫🇷 France
21. 🇦🇿 Azerbaijan
22. 🇳🇴 Norway
23. 🇳🇱 Netherlands
24. 🇮🇹 Italy
25. 🇸🇪 Sweden
26. 🇸🇲 San Marino
