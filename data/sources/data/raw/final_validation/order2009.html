Telefis 14 May 2009 9:08pm
Love the stage this year - LED screens and coordination superb. The overall form of the set is a tad predictable however - the curved wall background a staple of a number of years' shows now. The flooring, though simply spectacular as a display, is particularly lacklustre in form - the straight edge and lack of integration with the wider venue being remarkably crude. The generally flat floor offers little dynamisim or change in perspective for moving shots. The suspended elements are a delight.

Agreed with noggin (hey again for the annual Eurochat ) that the cut rate is a little slower this year - then again, is anyone as nifty as Sven?! The steadicam operation is spectacularly good -probably the best ever - however the general tone of the shooting script is a little on the nieve side, with certain resonances of Athens in its reliance on peds. This is also probably the first year ever where the jib arm has been relegated into second place for prestige shots, and indeed level of usage generally, since its introduction to the contest way back in 1988. Steadicam has well and truly, and somewhat tiresomely at times, taken over. It produces amazing visuals, but I continue to contend as ever, that it is over-used by about 25%. Thankfully however, thanks to its superb operation this year, its use is not a lazy padding tool as at previous contests. Quite sophisticated at Moscow.

The quality of camerawork for the Irish performance was abominable though! Truly, I think that was the worst shot sequence I have seen since 1993! All of the problems came from appalling ped operation: shots not composed in time, constant out of focus telephotos, shakes and loss of control, and in some cases an apparent lack of knowledge of what was even going on! Quite bizarre all-round, as the rest of the semi has shown none of these faults. Clearly they just can't handle rock-pop! What a mess - I literally cringed the whole way through it! Some great cutting and steadicam operation offered some consolation.

Also another little sign of inexperience, and a pet hate of many here , is the failure to get railcams and jibs moving before being cut up. Something that may yet be ironed out by Saturday...

Okay folks, I've now watched the results of both semis. Both my preferences and the actual results are shown, so you can compare them. My preferred countries that qualified are shown in bold.

Semi-Final 1
My preferred qualifiers: Montenegro, Belgium, Sweden, Andorra, Turkey, Romania, Finland, Malta, Bosnia & Herzegovina; plus either Belarus or Israel.

Actual results: Turkey, Sweden, Israel, Portugal, Malta, Finland, Bosnia & Herzegovina, Romania, Armenia, Iceland.

Thank heavens Sweden went through, as I'd have been gutted if that hadn't qualified. As for Turkey and Finland, didn't I tell you they'd be sure to go through, even though they weren't the ones I'd have voted for had I been watching live Tuesday evening. I'm disappointed that Belgium failed to qualify, as that was a lively Elvis Presley style song. Since the introduction of the semi-final in 2004, this was the year they most deserved to qualify, though I did wonder if it was too dated for Eurovision. Also I'm disappointed Montenegro and Andorra failed to qualify. I'd sooner Armenia had stayed out to let either Montenegro, Belgium or Andorra in. Remember though, I did say "Knowing my luck this (Armenia) will go through just for the dance routine". Also I wasn't overly keen on Iceland. I'd sooner Andorra, Belgium or Montenegro had gone through instead of them. Mind you, Iceland going through could be to the UK's advantage, because I think "My Time" is better than Iceland's song. Thank heavens Czech Republic and Bulgaria both failed to qualify as those were both rubbish unworthy of being in the final.

Semi-Final 2
My preferred qualifiers: Croatia, Ireland, Poland, Norway, Denmark, Greece, Albania, Estonia, Netherlands; plus either Hungary or Azerbaijan.

Actual results: Azerbaijan, Croatia, Ukraine, Lithuania, Albania, Moldova, Denmark, Estonia, Norway, Greece

I'm so overjoyed Norway went through, as I'd have been gutted if that hadn't qualified. Ditto Greece. Also I'm glad Croatia, Albania, Estonia and Denmark qualified. However I'm ever so disappointed that Ireland and Netherlands failed to qualify again. Both were great songs, though remember I did ask if this year's Netherlands entry "Shine" was too dated. Perhaps that was the case. I'd rather Ukraine hadn't qualified, but remember I did say last night "the big wheels will surely ensure it goes through against my wishes". As for Lithuania and Moldova, they shouldn't have qualified. I thought Lithuania was awful, and didn't deserve to qualify. Ireland and Netherlands deserved the places taken by Lithuania and Moldova. Thank God Latvia, Serbia, Slovakia and Slovenia all failed to make it to the final though.

Well now that we know the 25 countries in the final (Albania, Armenia, Azerbaijan, Bosnia & Herzegovina, Croatia, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Iceland, Israel, Lithuania, Malta, Moldova, Norway, Portugal, Romania, Russia, Spain, Sweden, Turkey, Ukraine, United Kingdom) who will win? Got a mix of pre-1992 and post 1992 countries in there, haven't we?

Let's look at some country groups:
Scandinavia: complete representation again, i.e. Denmark, Finland, Iceland, Norway, Sweden.
Former Yugoslavia: just two countries of six, i.e. Bosnia & Herzegovina, Croatia. The other four ex-Yugoslav countries (FYR Macedonia, Montenegro, Serbia, Slovenia) are all out. Still they'll all be able to give their 10s and 12s to Bosnia & Herzegovia and Croatia tomorrow night.
Baltic Area: we've got Estonia and Lithuania, but no Latvia.
Benelux: none. Belgium and Netherlands both failed to qualify. As I've already said, both those countries should have gone through, but then again, I did say both were perhaps a tad dated for Eurovision.
Greece and Cyprus: Greece are (deservedly) in, Cyprus are (thankfully) out. So Cyprus will give 12 to Greece, but who will Greece now give their 12 to?

Finally let's go right back to the seven founding countries of the very first Eurovision Song Contest in 1956, i.e. Netherlands, Switzerland, Belgium, Germany, France, Luxembourg, Italy.
Netherlands, Switzerland, Belgium: competed in semis, all failed to qualify.
Germany, France: direct qualifiers to final. Would they have qualified had they been forced to enter the semis?
Luxembourg, Italy: not entered for several years. Will they ever enter again?
