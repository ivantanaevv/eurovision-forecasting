FIGURES AND TABLES: SOURCE AND PURPOSE

1. data_song_patterns.pdf
   Untitled26 (1).ipynb, cells 3 and 6; same processed A data.
   Running-order association and overlap of three audio-feature distributions.
   Two original notebook plots are combined and relabelled in English.

2. data_country_geography.pdf
   Descriptive notebook, cells 4 and 5; processed B and C data.
   Country-level audience/jury differences and raw geographic associations.
   Year-bootstrap and Wilson calculations follow the notebook.

3. market_increment.pdf
   Development summaries from all three core/market model runs.
   Matched addition of the odds variable; quantified in the companion table.

4. annual_market_complete.pdf
   Saved market development_metrics_by_year.csv, A/B/C.
   Both losses shown in each notebook: A top-ten Brier and ranking log loss;
   B Brier and binary log loss; C Brier and categorical log loss.

5. forecast_ab_2025.pdf
   A/B market comparison_2025.csv and no-odds notebook HTML comparison tables.
   Shows which country probabilities change when odds enter M1.
   Core probabilities retain displayed precision of 0.1 percentage point.

6. forecast_c_2025.pdf
   C market comparison_2025.csv and no-odds notebook cell 19 HTML table.
   M2 probability of the actual national winner for every one of the 36 voters.
   This is a realized-outcome diagnostic, not the full forecast distribution.

7. pair_effects.pdf
   C market feature_effects_2025.csv, posterior fitted through 2024.
   Conditional coefficient magnitudes and 90% credible intervals.

8. calibration_market.pdf
   Saved A/B/C market calibration.csv.
   Year-weighted reliability bins; marker sizes reflect raw bin counts.
   A/B calibrate binary events; C calibrates top-choice correctness.

9. national_win_counts.pdf
   C market predictions_2025.csv and comparison_2025.csv.
   Sum of national probabilities versus realized counts for each recipient.
   Shows missed concentration in Israel and Estonia, without assuming that
   summing means produces a complete predictive count distribution.

10. A_core_annual_notebook.png, B_core_annual_notebook.png,
    C_core_annual_notebook.png
    Original embedded PNGs: A cell 17, B/C cell 15.
    Kept unchanged because machine-readable annual core metrics were not
    supplied. No values are inferred by digitizing the lines.

11. regional_pairs.pdf
    Descriptive notebook cell 9 calculation applied to its fixed ten-country
    regional subset. Outcome-selected extra countries are omitted, explicitly
    noted in the caption. Each label retains wins and eligible-year count.

DESCRIPTIVE TABLES
   Annual coverage replaces the descriptive notebook's coverage heat map:
   exact counts are more useful here than a second image of the same coverage.
   Feature summaries show the scale, dispersion and missing counts.
   The archived 2025 top-twelve market table replaces the notebook's market-rank
   diagram and preserves both normalized input probabilities and final places.

ADDITIONAL MODEL TABLES
   Full-ranking and overall-winner log losses for A.
   Top-one/top-three national-winner accuracy for C.
   Six no-odds feature-model prior-sensitivity comparisons for 2024.

NOT DUPLICATED IN THE MANUSCRIPT
   Individual MCMC trace plots: numerical diagnostics and convergence gates
   are already reported; a few visually selected chains add little evidence.
   Large C probability matrices: the all-voter realized-winner diagnostic and
   aggregate count plot give clearer explanations of the reported losses.
   Repeated core calibration screenshots: the main text includes the saved
   market calibration bins, with sparse-bin limitations stated explicitly.
   Original A/B forecast charts: replaced by a direct matched odds comparison
   of individual M1 forecasts; M0/M2 aggregate scores remain in the tables.

All numerical charts can be rebuilt from evidence/ using the two Python
scripts, except the three clearly identified original notebook PNGs.
The figure folder also contains PNG previews of the vector figures.
